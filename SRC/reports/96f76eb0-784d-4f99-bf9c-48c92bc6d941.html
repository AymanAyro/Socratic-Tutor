Concept diagram

Model answer

Foundation models are large-scale artificial intelligence models trained on vast, diverse datasets that can be adapted to a wide range of downstream tasks. Unlike traditional AI systems designed for single, specific functions, foundation models leverage self-supervised learning to develop broad, general-purpose representations of data. This creates a versatile base that can be refined through fine-tuning or prompting for specialized applications, such as natural language processing, image synthesis, or robotic control. This paradigm shifts AI development from building isolated models for every new problem to adapting a single, powerful foundation. Consequently, these models often exhibit emergent capabilities—skills not explicitly programmed during initial training—allowing them to generalize across domains and significantly reduce the amount of task-specific data required for high-performance deployment.

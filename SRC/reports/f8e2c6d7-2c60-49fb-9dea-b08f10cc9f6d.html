Model answer

Prompt engineering is the strategic process of designing, refining, and optimizing inputs to guide Large Language Models (LLMs) toward producing the most accurate, relevant, and high-quality outputs. This discipline involves applying specific techniques to constrain the model's probabilistic nature and align its responses with a precise user intent. Key methodologies include "few-shot prompting," where the model is provided with several examples of the desired task to establish a pattern, and "Chain-of-Thought" prompting, which encourages the model to decompose complex problems into intermediate reasoning steps. Furthermore, effective prompt engineering utilizes persona assignment, explicit output formatting, and iterative refinement to minimize hallucinations and ensure adherence to specific constraints. By optimizing the context and the specificity of the instructions, users can maximize the model's utility and reliability for specialized tasks without the need for fine-tuning the underlying architecture.

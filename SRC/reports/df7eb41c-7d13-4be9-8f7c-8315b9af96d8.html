Model answer

Prompt engineering is the iterative process of optimizing input text to guide large language models (LLMs) toward generating accurate, high-quality, and contextually relevant outputs. At an advanced level, this involves applying structured frameworks to constrain the model's probabilistic nature and improve its reasoning capabilities. Key techniques include few-shot prompting, where the model is provided with specific examples to establish a pattern, and chain-of-thought (CoT) prompting, which directs the model to decompose complex problems into intermediate logical steps. Furthermore, sophisticated prompt engineering utilizes system prompts to define a model's persona and behavioral constraints, alongside the use of delimiters to clearly separate instructions from data. By refining the specificity, context, and structural constraints of the input, engineers can significantly reduce hallucinations and increase the reliability and reproducibility of the model's performance across diverse and complex tasks.
